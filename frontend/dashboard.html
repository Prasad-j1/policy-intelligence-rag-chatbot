Why it's different

Built to be checked, not just trusted

Most AI assistants ask you to take their word for it. This one shows its work — every answer traces back to a real document, a real page, and a measured confidence score.

Grounded, evidence-backed answers

Every response is generated only from retrieved policy content — never invented. If the knowledge base doesn't have the answer, it says so instead of guessing.
